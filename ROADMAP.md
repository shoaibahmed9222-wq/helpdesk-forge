# Roadmap

1. **Skeleton** — Local Postgres with pgvector, Auth.js sign-in, workspaces, an empty dashboard, and a health check.
2. **Docs ingestion and embeddings** — Add a knowledge base and store embeddings.
3. **RAG chat API** — Answers with citations, behind a small LLM-provider adapter (bring your own key).
4. **Embeddable widget** — Chat widget for the business's site.
5. **Human handoff inbox** — A person picks up conversations the agent will not answer.
6. **Conversations and basic analytics** — History and simple counts.

Later:

- Email, Shopify, and Slack channels
- Stripe billing
- Marketing landing page + pricing page
- Team seats
